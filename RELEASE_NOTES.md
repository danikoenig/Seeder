## Seeder 1.0.0

MIDI generator by Dani Koenig. Play one note: it becomes the root and plants a seed. Chords, melody, arp and bass are generated from it. Another note changes everything.

- Mute (ON/OFF) and MIDI channel per layer
- Drag and drop: each layer (or all four) as a .mid file into your DAW
- Instance link: several instances share key, seed and settings; each plays its own role (Chords, Melody, Arp, Bass) on MIDI channel 1
- Mood (6) and Style (7), Complexity, Density, Swing, Variation, Seed
- Design like Repetitor, resizable, opens at 100 %
- VST3, AU and CLAP, installer and uninstaller (.pkg)

Install: download `Seeder-Installer.pkg` (see INSTALL.txt in the zip). Apple Silicon, macOS 11 or newer.
