## Seeder 1.1.0

- 7 layers: new Stabs (short chord hits), Pad (held chords) and Counter (a second line that answers the melody); each with mute, MIDI channel, drag and drop and link role
- Phrase length 4, 8 or 16 bars: 8 and 16 bars answer themselves (the second half repeats and varies the first). Default 8
- 10 moods, sorted from sad to bright: Tragic, Dark, Melancholic, Nostalgic, Hypnotic, Jazzy, Dreamy, Soulful, Happy, Euphoric
- 12 styles: new Piano House, Tech House, Afro House, Progressive and Acid House
- New PHRASE section: chord change (bar, half bar, 2 bars), voicing, arp pattern and rate, bass movement (root, octaves, walking), fill, humanize
- Link: the new options are shared too. Instances of 1.0 and 1.1 do not link with each other (update all instances)
- Saved projects from 1.0: layer order and mood list changed, so old presets sound different

Install: download `Seeder-Installer.pkg` (see INSTALL.txt in the zip). Apple Silicon, macOS 11 or newer.
