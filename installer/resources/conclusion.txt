Restart your DAW and rescan plugins. Then search for "Seeder" in your plugin list
and put it before an instrument (Ableton Live: use "Seeder Instrument" on its own
MIDI track, other tracks take it via "MIDI From").
Logic Pro: the plugin is under MIDI FX > Audio Units > Dani Koenig.

Several instances? Leave LINK on: they share the key, the seed and the settings.
Give each instance its own ROLE (Chords, Melody, Arp, Bass).
