Seeder - MIDI generator (MIDI effect and instrument)
by Dani Koenig

Play one note: it becomes the root and plants a seed. Chords, melody, arp and bass
are generated from it. Play another note and everything changes.

This installs Seeder and Seeder Instrument as:
- VST3:       /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3         (Bitwig, Ableton Live, Cubase, Reaper ...)
- Audio Unit: /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components   (Logic Pro, GarageBand)
- CLAP:       /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP         (Bitwig, Reaper ...)

Requirements: Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer), macOS 11 or newer.
